# Inventory Management System - Client Overview

## Executive Summary

Our Inventory Management System is a comprehensive business solution designed to streamline your inventory operations, improve efficiency, and provide complete visibility into your business processes. The system is built with multi-tenant architecture, ensuring that each business admin has their own secure, isolated workspace.

## Key Business Benefits

✅ **Complete Business Isolation** - Each admin gets their own dedicated workspace  
✅ **Real-time Inventory Tracking** - Know exactly what you have in stock at any moment  
✅ **Automated Stock Alerts** - Never run out of critical inventory again  
✅ **Financial Transparency** - Track all payments, expenses, and profits in one place  
✅ **Multi-user Access** - Admin and staff can work together efficiently  
✅ **Comprehensive Reporting** - Make data-driven business decisions  

---

## Core Features

### 1. **Business Management**
- **Individual Business Workspace**: Each admin gets their own isolated business environment
- **Business Profile Management**: Store your company information, branding, and settings
- **Multi-location Support**: Manage inventory across different locations if needed

### 2. **User & Contact Management**
- **Role-based Access Control**: 
  - **Admin**: Full system access and control
  - **Staff**: Limited access for day-to-day operations
- **Contact Management**: Maintain comprehensive records of:
  - **Suppliers**: Your vendors and their contact information
  - **Customers**: Retailers, dealers, wholesalers, and regular customers
  - **Priority Classifications**: Mark important contacts for special treatment
- **Customer Balance Tracking**: Monitor who owes you money and whom you owe

### 3. **Product Catalog Management**
- **Comprehensive Product Database**:
  - Product names, descriptions, and images
  - SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) management
  - Purchase and selling prices
  - Current stock levels
- **Product Organization**:
  - **Categories**: Group similar products (e.g., Electronics, Clothing)
  - **Brands**: Track products by manufacturer
  - **Units**: Define how products are measured (pieces, kg, liters, etc.)
- **Stock Alerts**: Set low-stock thresholds to get automatic warnings

### 4. **Purchase Order Management**
- **Supplier Orders**: Create and manage orders to your suppliers
- **Order Tracking**: Monitor order status from placement to delivery
- **Partial Deliveries**: Handle situations where suppliers deliver in multiple shipments
- **Cost Management**: Track purchase prices and manage supplier payments
- **Delivery Planning**: Set expected delivery dates and track performance

### 5. **Sales Order Management**
- **Customer Orders**: Process sales to all types of customers
- **Invoice Generation**: Automatic invoice numbering and management
- **Order Status Tracking**: From order placement to delivery
- **Pricing Control**: Manage selling prices and apply discounts
- **Tax Management**: Handle VAT/GST calculations automatically

### 6. **Inventory Control**
- **Real-time Stock Levels**: Always know your current inventory
- **Automatic Stock Updates**: Inventory adjusts automatically with each sale or purchase
- **Stock Movement History**: Complete audit trail of every stock change
- **Stock Adjustments**: Manual corrections when needed (with proper documentation)
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Proactive notifications before you run out

### 7. **Financial Management**
- **Payment Tracking**: 
  - Track all payments received from customers
  - Monitor payments made to suppliers
  - Support multiple payment methods (cash, bank transfer, cheque, etc.)
- **Outstanding Balances**: 
  - See who owes you money (receivables)
  - Track what you owe to suppliers (payables)
- **Payment Methods**: Configure your preferred payment gateways and bank accounts
- **Cheque Management**: Special handling for cheque payments with status tracking

### 8. **Expense Management**
- **Business Expenses**: Track all your business costs
- **Expense Categories**: Organize expenses by type (rent, utilities, marketing, etc.)
- **Reference Numbers**: Link expenses to invoices or receipts
- **Monthly/Annual Reporting**: Understand your spending patterns

### 9. **Employee Management**
- **Staff Records**: Maintain employee information and contact details
- **Salary Management**: Track employee salaries and advances
- **User Permissions**: Control what each employee can access in the system

### 10. **Investment & Income Tracking**
- **Investor Management**: Keep records of business investors
- **Investment Tracking**: Monitor capital investments and their status
- **Additional Income**: Track other revenue sources beyond product sales
- **Income Categories**: Organize different types of income

## Business Process Flow

### **Purchase Process**
1. **Create Purchase Order** → Send to supplier
2. **Receive Goods** → Update inventory automatically
3. **Process Payment** → Track supplier payments
4. **Generate Reports** → Analyze purchase costs

### **Sales Process**
1. **Create Sales Order** → Generate invoice
2. **Process Sale** → Automatically reduce inventory
3. **Receive Payment** → Update customer balance
4. **Delivery Tracking** → Monitor order fulfillment

### **Inventory Monitoring**
1. **Real-time Updates** → Stock levels change automatically
2. **Low Stock Alerts** → Get notified before running out
3. **Stock Adjustments** → Make corrections when needed
4. **Audit Trail** → Every change is recorded
